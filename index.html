Turn a link into a video file.

A small Windows app built around yt-dlp and FFmpeg. Paste one link or a whole list, choose the quality, and it downloads, merges, and remembers everything you've pulled down.

What it actually does

Batch downloads
Drop in a list of links, or point it at a .txt file, and it queues every one of them.
Your call on quality
Pick the exact resolution and audio track, or let it grab the best combination automatically.
A history that remembers
Every attempt — finished, failed, or canceled — is logged locally. Search it, redownload something, or jump straight to the original page.
Looks how you want
Light, dark, or matching Windows. Switches instantly, no restart.
English and Polish
Follows your system language on first run, or pick one yourself.
